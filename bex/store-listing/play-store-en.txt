# Passla — Play Store Listing (English)

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Complete tasks, earn rewards. Find local business tasks and win coupons.

## Full description

Earn rewards by completing tasks from local businesses with Passla!

**What you can do**
• Discover tasks near you
• Complete tasks and earn coupons
• Message businesses and send offers
• Trade skills and collaborate
• Add a bio and optional CV to your profile
• Redeem coupons via QR code

**Who is it for?**
Students, freelancers, and anyone looking for extra income. Businesses post tasks; you complete them.

**Your privacy**
Location is used only while the app is open to show nearby tasks.
